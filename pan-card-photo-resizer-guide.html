1. Why PAN Portal Applications Get Rejected

Online PAN card applications filed through NSDL (Protean eGov) or UTIITSL frequently fail during document verification due to subtle image mismatches. Over 70% of portal rejections stem from incorrect physical dimensions, missing EXIF DPI resolution tags, or files slightly exceeding 50 KB.

2. Official NSDL vs. UTIITSL Specification Matrix

Agency Portal Document Type Dimensions DPI Resolution Max File Size
NSDL (Protean) Passport Photo 3.5 cm x 2.5 cm (213x197 px) 200 DPI ≤ 50 KB
NSDL (Protean) Signature 2.0 cm x 4.5 cm (157x354 px) 200 DPI ≤ 50 KB
UTIITSL Passport Photo 213 x 213 pixels 300 DPI ≤ 30 KB
UTIITSL Signature 400 x 200 pixels 600 DPI ≤ 60 KB

3. Signature Scanning Rules: Black Ink vs. Blue Ink

Government scanning algorithms run high-contrast edge detection. Signing with black ballpoint pen on plain unlined white paper provides crisp, solid contrast. Blue ink or pencil lines often drop out during automated portal scanning, causing verification errors.
