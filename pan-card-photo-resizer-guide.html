2. Top 3 Reasons PAN Applications Get Rejected

  • Incorrect DPI or Pixel Size: Submitting a random square photo without 300 DPI metadata causes the portal validator to reject the upload.
  • Blue Ink or Gel Pen Signature: Both NSDL and UTIITSL require crisp black ballpoint ink for biometric signature clarity.
  • Blurry or Selfies: Selfies with busy backgrounds, glare on spectacles, or shadows behind ears are strictly rejected.
