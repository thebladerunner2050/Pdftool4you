1. Why Watermarking PDFs Is Essential for Businesses & Creators

Whether you are distributing confidential financial audits, preliminary architectural blueprints, academic research drafts, or legal contracts, watermarking is the primary line of defense against unauthorized sharing and copyright infringement.

A clear semi-transparent overlay (e.g., "CONFIDENTIAL", "SAMPLE", or "DO NOT COPY") signals document status while preventing recipient organizations from representing the draft as a finalized copy.

2. Text Watermarks vs. Logo Image Watermarks

Text Typography Watermarks

Vector Font Superimposition

Directly embeds vector glyphs into the PDF stream. Font size, hex color, rotation angle (-90° to +90°), and alpha transparency (5% to 100%) can be fine-tuned.

Logo Image Stamps

Transparent PNG & JPG Brand Stamps

Embeds corporate logos or rubber stamps into the document. Transparent PNGs preserve smooth antialiased edges without white background boxes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will watermarking cover up the underlying document text?

No. By choosing an opacity setting between 15% and 25%, the watermark is drawn in a semi-transparent layer over the page, allowing all original typography and vector lines to remain clearly readable.

Can I apply watermarks to specific pages only?

Yes! Under Page Range Settings, select "All Pages" or "First Page Only".
