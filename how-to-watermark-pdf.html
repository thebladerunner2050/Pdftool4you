1. Why Document Watermarking Is Critical

When sharing draft agreements, financial projections, intellectual property, or confidential client memos, watermarking serves as the primary visual defense against unauthorized duplication, leaks, and plagiarism.

Common watermark markers include:

  • CONFIDENTIAL / STRICTLY PRIVATE: Informs recipients that the document is sensitive.
  • DRAFT / SAMPLE: Prevents premature execution of unfinished contracts.
  • DO NOT COPY: Discourages unauthorized distribution during review stages.
  • COMPANY LOGO / BRAND MARK: Establishes clear copyright ownership on whitepapers and reports.

2. Best Practices for Professional Watermarking

1. Opacity Level

Use 20% to 30% opacity. This ensures the watermark is distinct while keeping the underlying text fully legible.

2. -45° Diagonal Angle

A -45° diagonal angle spans across multiple text lines, making removal via cropping practically impossible.

3. Transparent PNG Logos

When using image stamps, upload transparent PNG assets to avoid unsightly solid white bounding boxes.
