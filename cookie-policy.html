1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you load web pages in your browser. They are widely used by web developers to make websites run efficiently, enhance user experience, remember theme preferences, and provide analytical reporting.

Along with cookies, modern browsers support local storage (localStorage), which stores preferences without sending data across network request headers.

4. How to Manage Cookies in Your Web Browser

Most desktop and mobile browsers allow you to modify cookie settings. You can configure your browser to reject all cookies or notify you before a cookie is stored:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Apple Safari: Settings → Safari → Advanced → Block All Cookies.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.
