Frequently Asked Questions

Specific queries about PAN card photo and signature submission.

What is the official size requirement for NSDL photo?

For NSDL applications, the passport photo must be exactly 3.5 cm x 2.5 cm, with a scanning resolution of 200 DPI. The resulting file size should be strictly under 50 KB in JPEG format.

Why does my signature need to be in black ink?

Online verification systems run high-contrast scanning. Black ballpoint ink on clear white paper creates the highest contrast, preventing blurry borders and eliminating rejection risk. Blue ink or pencil marks can fade or drop out during system processing.

Does UTIITSL use the same requirements as NSDL?

No. UTIITSL has distinct specifications. Photographs must be precisely 213 x 213 pixels (at 300 DPI) and under 30 KB. Signatures must be 400 x 200 pixels (at 600 DPI) and under 60 KB. This tool automatically shifts presets based on your portal selection.
