Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how PDF page rotation works.

Does rotating a PDF document reduce text or image quality?

No. PDFTool4You modifies the page viewport rotation matrix directly inside the PDF metadata specification using vector transformations. Text, fonts, images, and graphics remain 100% loss-free and crisp.

Can I rotate single upside-down pages individually?

Yes! Each page thumbnail card in the visual editor has individual ↶ -90°, ↷ +90°, and 180° rotation buttons. You can rotate individual pages or use bulk controls for even, odd, or all pages simultaneously.

Are my uploaded PDF files safe and private?

Yes. PDFTool4You processes all documents completely inside your web browser's local memory. Your files are never sent to external servers.
