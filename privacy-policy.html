Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026 · Effective immediately across all PDFTool4You services.

Our Core Privacy Principle: Zero Server File Uploads

Unlike traditional file conversion websites, PDFTool4You performs all PDF and image transformations (compressing, merging, splitting, watermarking, format conversion) 100% locally in your web browser. Your confidential files, photos, legal contracts, and sensitive personal records are never uploaded to, transmitted across, or stored on our servers.

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to PDFTool4You ("we", "us", "our", or "the Service"). We are committed to safeguarding the digital privacy and personal data of every individual who accesses our website at pdftool4you.in. This Privacy Policy clarifies how information is gathered, utilized, disclosed, and protected when you interact with our suite of client-side document utilities.

By accessing or using PDFTool4You, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you disagree with any segment of this policy, we advise you to discontinue use of the site.

2. File Data & Local Browser Architecture

The cornerstone of PDFTool4You is privacy by design:

  • No Server Upload: When you select a file (PDF, JPG, PNG) using our file drop areas, the file is read into your browser's private local memory (HTML5 File API, JavaScript ArrayBuffer, and HTML Canvas).
  • Local Processing: All compression algorithms, page merging logic, page splitting, and watermarking execute solely on your device CPU/GPU.
  • Immediate Disposal: Once you close the tab or reload the window, all file data held in volatile browser memory is immediately freed by your browser's garbage collector.

4. Cookies & Web Storage Technologies

Cookies are minute text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. PDFTool4You uses cookies and browser local storage strictly for functional and advertising purposes:

  • Functional Local Storage: We use your browser's localStorage to remember your theme choice (System Default, Light, or Dark mode) and your cookie preferences. No personal identity is stored.
  • Advertising & Analytics Cookies: Third-party partners like Google AdSense set cookies to deliver relevant advertisements, track campaign effectiveness, and prevent fraudulent impressions or click spam.

You can instruct your web browser to refuse all cookies or to alert you when a cookie is being transmitted. However, certain advertising preference options or UI settings may not persist if cookies and web storage are completely blocked.

5. Log Files & Diagnostic Data

Like standard web hosting environments, our web servers may automatically log standard request metadata when your browser requests website assets (such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript files, or fonts). These server logs may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses

This standard diagnostic data is utilized exclusively for server performance monitoring, DDoS mitigation, network security, and infrastructure administration. It is never associated with any user document content.

  • Right to Erasure: You may request the deletion of your personal contact data or communication history under applicable legal conditions.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You may object to or restrict processing of your data, including personalized advertising cookies.
