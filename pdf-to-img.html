Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between 150 DPI and 300 DPI?

150 DPI offers standard clarity with smaller image file sizes suitable for digital sharing. 300 DPI provides double pixel density for crisp typography and high-definition printing.

Can I extract only specific pages from a long PDF?

Yes! In the "Pages to Convert" field, enter specific page numbers or ranges (e.g. 1, 3, 5-8) to convert only the exact pages you need.

Are my sensitive documents safe?

Yes, 100%. The conversion runs completely inside your web browser. No PDF file or extracted image is ever transmitted across the network or stored on any server.
