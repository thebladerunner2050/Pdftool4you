1. Format Comparison: WebP, PNG, and JPG

PNG (Portable Network Graphics)

Lossless compression supporting 8-bit alpha transparency. Best for signatures, watermarks, logos, and crisp text charts.

JPG / JPEG

Lossy discrete cosine transform compression. Best for continuous-tone photography, ID card photos, and scanned certificates.

WebP

Next-gen Google format with 30% smaller footprint. Converting WebP to JPG ensures universal compatibility across legacy portals.
