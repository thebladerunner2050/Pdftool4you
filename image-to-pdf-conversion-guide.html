1. Why Convert Pictures to PDF Documents?

Converting loose camera photos, scanned document snapshots, identity cards, or receipts into a single PDF document creates a professional, structured package. Government submission portals, college applications, and tax agencies accept multi-page PDFs far more reliably than raw image files.

2. Image Format Comparison: JPG vs. PNG vs. WebP

Format Compression Type Transparency Support Best Use Case
JPG / JPEG Lossy (DCTDecode) No (Opaque) Camera photos, scanned receipts, documents
PNG Lossless (FlateDecode) Yes (Alpha Channel) Logos, charts, diagrams, text screenshots
WebP Modern Lossy/Lossless Yes (Alpha Channel) Web graphics, mobile phone snapshots

3. Understanding Page Layout & Padding Margins

When compiling images into PDF, choosing page size determines paper bounds:

A4 Standard Print

210 x 297 mm. The universal standard for office printing, resumes, and official applications.

US Letter

8.5 x 11 inches. Standard paper size for North American corporate documentation.

Fit to Image

Dynamically sets page bounds to match exact photo dimensions without padding margins.
