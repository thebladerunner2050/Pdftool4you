Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how browser-side PDF cropping and printing work.

How does direct mobile printing work?

When you tap Print PDF, PDFTool4You renders your cropped pages in full resolution and opens your phone or computer's native system print dialog. You can print to AirPrint, AirPrint Wi-Fi printers, Android Print Service, or save directly as PDF.

Is PDF cropping easy on mobile touchscreens?

Yes! Our crop box features enlarged touch target hitboxes on corner handles. You can drag handles with your thumb, adjust margin sliders, or tap ⚡ Auto-Trim to automatically crop blank white margins.

Does cropping a PDF reduce image or text quality?

No. Unlike image editors that rasterize entire documents, PDFTool4You updates vector CropBox parameters inside the PDF specification. Text and images remain 100% loss-free.
