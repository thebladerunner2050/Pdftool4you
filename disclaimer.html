2. File Conversion & Technical Nuances

Portable Document Format (PDF) files are inherently complex structured documents that may contain embedded TrueType/OpenType fonts, encrypted object streams, ICC color profiles, digital cryptographic signatures, layers, and interactive form fields.

  • Compression Artifacts: Reducing PDF file size through image re-encoding or vector simplification can result in visible loss of high-resolution details or sharpness. You must inspect the generated document prior to publishing or printing.
  • Font Substitution: If an input PDF references non-embedded or restricted system fonts, your browser rendering engine may substitute fallback typefaces during rendering.
  • Digital Signatures: Modifying, splitting, or watermarking a cryptographically signed PDF may invalidate pre-existing digital signatures or certificates.
  • Always Maintain Backups: Always preserve original, unmodified copies of your files prior to processing them with our compression, merging, splitting, or watermarking utilities.
